Get Ready Before a Storm

Preparation before a storm event reduces risk and response time when conditions deteriorate quickly.

Information & planning
Stay connected
  • Monitor the Cape Storm Monitor and SA Weather Service for watches and warnings.
  • Save this page to your home screen for fast access.
Prepare essentials
  • Keep your phone charged and save emergency contacts.
  • Assemble a small bag with water, medication, ID, warm clothing, and a torch.
Secure your home
Protect the property
  • Bring in or secure loose outdoor items such as furniture, planters, and tools.
  • Clear gutters and drains so water can flow away safely.
Get Set Watch & Warning

A warning has been issued. Conditions are deteriorating or imminent. Reduce exposure and prepare to act.

Stay informed
Keep monitoring
  • Check the incident map and weather updates regularly.
  • Expect conditions to deteriorate quickly once a warning is issued.
Avoid hazards
  • Stay away from rivers, stormwater channels, and coastal areas.
  • Do not approach fallen trees, damaged power lines, or weakened structures.
Go Flooding & Evacuation
If you are in immediate danger, call 107 or 10111 now. Do not attempt dangerous crossings. Do not wait for water to recede before calling for help.
Emergency actions
Floodwater safety
  • Never drive or walk through floodwater. Even shallow water can be deadly.
  • If your car stalls in rising water, leave it and move to higher ground immediately.
Evacuation
  • If ordered to evacuate, leave without delay with your go-bag, medications, and ID.
  • In informal settlements, move to higher ground or a designated shelter as soon as water rises.
Family safety
Protect people
  • Keep children away from stormwater canals and drainage channels.
  • Check on elderly or vulnerable neighbours if you can do so safely.
After Post Storm

Hazards persist after a storm passes. Do not assume conditions are safe until officially confirmed.

Recovery
Post-storm safety
  • Do not return to evacuated areas until the all-clear is given.
  • Avoid floodwater contact — it may contain sewage and chemicals.
Damage & services
  • Document damage before repairs for insurance.
  • Report outages to authorities; do not attempt electrical repairs yourself.
